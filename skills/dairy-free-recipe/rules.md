# Dairy-free rules

Cris's own dairy-free conversion rules, translated from her Notion page ("Reglas"). `SKILL.md` reads this file before every conversion, and it is the only source for ratios: change rules here, not in `SKILL.md`. The gluten-free rules from the same page live in the separate `gluten-free-recipe` skill.

"Plant milk" means almond, rice, soy or oat milk. Never use canned coconut milk.

## Butter cakes

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil and 15% plant milk.
3. Replace yogurt or sour cream with the same weight or volume of plain, unsweetened plant yogurt.
4. Replace buttermilk with vegan buttermilk.

## Brownies

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.

## Chocolate cakes with melted chocolate in the batter

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil and 15% plant milk.
3. Replace yogurt or sour cream with the same weight or volume of plain, unsweetened plant yogurt.
4. Replace buttermilk with vegan buttermilk.

## Swiss rolls and sponge cakes

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil.
3. Replace yogurt or sour cream with the same weight or volume of plain, unsweetened plant yogurt.
4. Replace buttermilk with vegan buttermilk.

## Cookies that hold their shape when baked

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.

## Cookies that spread when baked

1. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.

## Cake-like cookies

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.
3. Replace yogurt or sour cream with the same weight or volume of plain, unsweetened plant yogurt.
4. Replace buttermilk with vegan buttermilk.

## Shortcrust pastry

1. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.
2. Replace 20% of the flour with the same weight of starch (cornstarch, potato starch, tapioca starch or arrowroot).

## Puff and flaky pastry

1. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
2. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil and 15% plant milk.
3. Replace yogurt or sour cream with the same weight or volume of plain, unsweetened plant yogurt.
4. Replace buttermilk with vegan buttermilk.

## Thick pancakes

1. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil and 15% plant milk.
2. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
3. Increase the sugar by 5–10 g per 240 g of plant milk.

## Fluffy pancakes

1. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter. Or replace the butter's weight with 80% sunflower oil and 15% plant milk.
2. Replace the milk with the same weight or volume of plant milk.
3. Replace buttermilk with the same weight or volume of vegan buttermilk.
4. Increase the sugar by 10 g per 240 g of plant milk.

## Baked cheesecake

1. Replace the butter with the same weight or volume of vegan butter.
2. Replace the cream cheese with the same weight or volume of a vegan cream cheese that is low in water and has a texture like cream cheese. If it is too runny, strain it through cheesecloth.
3. Replace the yogurt with the same weight of vegan Greek yogurt.
4. Replace the cream with the same amount of vegan cream.
5. Add 5 g cornstarch per 225 g of cream cheese.
6. If the recipe has whipping cream, add 10 g cornstarch per 230 g of cream.
