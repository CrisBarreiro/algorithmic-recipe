Prepare the panGrease an 8x8-inch pan with butter, then dust it with flour.
PreheatHeat the oven to 350°F (170°C).
4 oz (115 g) unsalted butter MeltMelt the butter. MixStir in the sugar, vanilla and espresso. MixAdd the eggs and mix. Fold inFold in the flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt until combined. BakeBake at 350°F (170°C) for 30 to 40 min.
1 cup (200 g) sugar
1/4 tsp (2.5 mL) vanilla extract
1 shot (4 tbsp; 60 mL) fresh brewed espresso or very strong coffee
2 large (100 g) eggs
1/2 cup (80 g) all-purpose flour
1/3 cup (80 g) Hershey's cocoa powder
1/4 tsp (1.3 g) baking soda
1/4 tsp (1.5 g) table salt
