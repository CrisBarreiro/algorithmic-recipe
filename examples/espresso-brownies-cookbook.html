Espresso brownies

Yield
one 8 × 8 in / 20 × 20 cm pan
Time estimate
30 to 40 minutes baking
Level of difficulty
easy
Special requirements
8 × 8 in baking pan

A one-bowl brownie where a shot of espresso does the heavy lifting: it does not make the brownies taste of coffee so much as make the cocoa taste darker and deeper.

Everything goes into the melted butter in turn, so there is only one bowl to wash. Fold the dry ingredients in just until no streaks remain; working the batter more makes the brownies cakey.
